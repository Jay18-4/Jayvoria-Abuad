## Brand & Style

This design system embodies culinary craftsmanship, quiet luxury, and operational precision. Created for modern, high-end commercial dining spaces and intelligent management suites, it balances the sensory warmth of fine dining with the ergonomic speed demanded by fast-paced restaurant operations.

### Personality & Emotional Tenor
- **Epicurean & Inviting:** Rich, appetite-piquing tones that make dishes feel artisanal, fresh, and meticulously prepared.
- **Editorial & Elevated:** Evoking boutique hospitality, Michelin-grade menus, and architectural interior dining spaces.
- **Instrument-Grade Precision:** Confident, swift, and highly legible across point-of-sale tablets, guest-facing digital menus, kitchen stations, and analytical back-office dashboards.

### Aesthetic Approach
A hybrid of **Warm Editorial Luxury** and **Modern Functional Minimalism**:
- Silky, non-sterile neutral foundations (warm creams, ivory stone, and deep slate charcoal) eliminate harsh blue-light glare in low-lit dining rooms.
- Micro-delight and tactile responsiveness: pill buttons with dynamic feedback, clean linear card borders, and high-legibility typographic hierarchies that allow vibrant photography and dish descriptions to shine without cognitive friction.

## Colors

The palette is tuned around culinary cues: radiant embers, warm saffron spices, crisp garden herbs, and charcoal slateware.

### Core Palettes
- **Primary (Ember Terracotta - `#E05A36`):** The signature accent for primary interactive elements, order callouts, cart confirmations, and active states. Deep ember (`#C84624`) serves as the hover and active press state.
- **Secondary (Warm Saffron / Honey - `#F59E0B`):** Highlights curation markers such as "Chef’s Selection", sommelier pairings, and promotional highlights.
- **Tertiary (Fresh Sage Herb - `#10B981` / `#059669`):** Represents organic, dietary-positive, and completed operational states (e.g., "Ready for Pickup", "Vegan", "Paid").
- **Neutral Core (Slate Charcoal - `#0F172A` / `#1E293B`):** Dense, architectural darks used for primary typography, headers, and POS high-contrast chrome.

### Surface Tones & Canvases
- **App Canvas (Velvety Warm Cream):** `#FAF8F5` base background; `#F5F2EB` for secondary surface wells and background panels.
- **Card & Sheet Elevation:** Pure crisp white (`#FFFFFF`) to ensure food photography pops with unadulterated color balance.
- **Delicate Dividers & Outlines:** Warm bone line (`#EAE5DC`) providing razor-sharp structure without the cold synthetic tint of raw zinc or gray.

## Typography

Typography acts as the bridge between hospitality storytelling and operational clarity.

- **Headlines & Editorial Accents (`Playfair Display`):** Reserved for category titles, tasting menu headers, signature dish titles, and promotional hero units. Conveys the legacy and refinement of fine dining.
- **Interface, Numbers & Operational Data (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Selected for its humanist warmth and geometric balance. Used across all interaction touchpoints, metadata, kitchen orders, ingredient lists, table numbers, and fiscal summaries.
- **Numerics & Currency:** Prices and inventory totals are set in semi-bold and bold weights of `Plus Jakarta Sans` with tabular figures enabled (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to prevent alignment wobble across bills, checkouts, and sales reports.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout system balances fluid browsing on mobile/tablet devices with dense, organized clarity for administrative and kitchen management.

### Grid Rhythm & Responsive Rules
- **Desktop & POS Terminals (1024px+):** 12-column fluid grid system with `2.5rem` canvas margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Admin data tables, floorplans, and dual-pane order queues maximize horizontal real estate.
- **Tablets & Waitstaff Handhelds (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout. Employs split-view interfaces (menu navigation on left 60%, real-time order ticket on right 40%).
- **Mobile Guest Ordering (< 768px):** 4-column layout with `1rem` margins and gutters. Single-column vertical stream with horizontal snapping scrollers for dietary categories and quick filters.

### Vertical Rhythm
Built on a consistent 4px/8px modular base. Elements must use structural whitespace rather than excessive container borders to establish grouping.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy pairs clean tonal layering with warm-tinted ambient illumination, steering clear of stark, cold drop shadows.

### Surface Hierarchy
1. **Base Plane (`#FAF8F5`):** Root background tone.
2. **Sub-Surface / Inset Wells (`#F5F2EB`):** Used for search bars, quantity adjustment trays, disabled surfaces, and receipt summaries.
3. **Card & Sheet Elevation (`#FFFFFF`):** Base container for dishes, modal dialogs, and table rows. Outlined with a continuous 1px warm border (`#EAE5DC`).

### Ambient Shadows
- **Card Rest:** `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.04), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(224, 90, 54, 0.03)`
- **Hover & Interactive Rise:** `0 12px 30px -4px rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(224, 90, 54, 0.06)`
- **Floating Cart & Sticky Overlays:** Frosted floating surfaces utilize `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` with `background: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.92)` combined with deep elevation: `0 20px 40px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.16)`.

## Shapes

The geometric signature uses roundedness level `2` (medium-high rounded curves), which softens technical interfaces and harmonizes with the culinary aesthetic.

- **Food Cards & Modal Sheets:** Utilize `rounded-xl` (1.5rem / 24px) to present imagery with organic framing.
- **Standard Controls (Inputs, Dropdowns, Segmented Tabs):** Utilize `rounded-md` (0.5rem / 8px) or `rounded-lg` (1rem / 16px) for approachable touch targets.
- **Pill Geometry (`rounded-full`):** Strictly reserved for dynamic badges, floating cart pills, counter buttons, and dietary indicators.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Ember Pill):** Solid `#E05A36` background, crisp `#FFFFFF` text, `rounded-full` or `rounded-lg`, font weight 600. Active press shifts to `#C84624`. Focus state displays an outer 3px ring of `#E05A36` at 30% opacity with a 2px white offset.
- **Secondary (Ivory Warm):** Background `#FFFFFF`, 1px border `#EAE5DC`, text `#1E293B`. Hover transforms background to `#FAF8F5` with border `#D6CEC2`.
- **Ghost/Tertiary:** No border, transparent background, text `#1E293B`, hover background `#F5F2EB`.

### Food Cards
- Crisp white background (`#FFFFFF`) framed by a subtle 1px border (`#EAE5DC`), `rounded-xl` (24px).
- Image occupies the top half with an edge-to-edge aspect ratio of 4:3 or 16:9, featuring a gentle zoom transition on hover.
- Badges float anchored 12px from the top-left corner. Price is anchored at the bottom right in high-contrast slate (`#0F172A`).
- Quick-add floating action button (+) nestled in the bottom right corner with interactive feedback.

### Badge Tags & Dietary Chips
- `rounded-full` pill structure with `padding: 4px 10px`, typography `label-sm` uppercase.
- **Chef's Special / Popular:** Background `rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.12)`, text `#B45309`, border `1px solid rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.25)`.
- **Vegan / Gluten-Free / Fresh:** Background `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.12)`, text `#047857`, border `1px solid rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.25)`.
- **Spicy / Hot:** Background `rgba(224, 90, 54, 0.12)`, text `#C84624`, border `1px solid rgba(224, 90, 54, 0.25)`.

### Interactive Quantity Pickers
- Pill container (`#F5F2EB`) with three segmented parts: minus button, numeric count, plus button.
- Buttons feature tactile press states with `#FFFFFF` circular backgrounds when active.
- Numbers switch seamlessly with zero layout shifting via fixed min-width tab stops.

### Floating Cart Pill
- Sticky bottom center (guest viewport) or bottom right (POS floor view).
- Rich slate charcoal background (`#0F172A`) or deep ember (`#E05A36`), with white typography and a saffron badge indicator displaying item count.
- Encased in ambient drop elevation with smooth slide-up reveal animations on item additions.

### Admin Data Tables & Status Chips
- Clean horizontal gridlines using `#EAE5DC`, avoiding vertical dividers.
- Header row styled in `label-md` with slate secondary coloring (`#64748B`), capitalized, with sort glyphs.
- Alternating subtle hover highlighting (`#FAF8F5`).
- Order Status Indicators:
  - *Incoming / Pending:* Honey pulse chip (`#FEF3C7` / `#92400E`).
  - *In Kitchen / Cooking:* Terracotta ember chip (`#FFEDD5` / `#9A3412`).
  - *Completed / Served:* Herb sage chip (`#D1FAE5` / `#065F46`).

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- Background `#FFFFFF`, border 1px `#EAE5DC`, `rounded-lg` (10px).
- Placeholder text in warm muted slate (`#94A3B8`).
- Focus ring: Smooth transition to border `#E05A36` with an outer glow: `box-shadow: 0 0 0 4px rgba(224, 90, 54, 0.12)`.
